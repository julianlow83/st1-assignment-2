# Requirements Review

## 1. Highlighted Nouns, Verbs, and Business Rules

### Nouns
* **Entities:** Patient, Healthcare Practitioner / GP, Receptionist / Staff, Clinic Management, Junior Software Engineer.
* **Objects:** Information, Records (Paper records, Patient records), Spreadsheet, Appointment (Bookings, History, Status, Availability), Report (Operational reports), Software System / Prototype.

### Verbs
* **Current Actions:** Manages, Provides (consultations), Experiences (problems), Uses (spreadsheets/paper).
* **Desired Capabilities:** Support, Replace (manual/fragmented processes), Develop (iteratively), Enhance, Book / Schedule, Locate, Cancel, Produce (operational reports).

### Business Rules
* **Scope Constraint:** The initial version must be a simple, manageable application for a small clinic not a complex hospital information system.
* **Development Approach:** System development must be iterative and completed in multiple stages with incremental prototype enhancements.
* **Scheduling Rules:** Appointments must be tracked without allowing duplicate bookings.
* **Data Integrity Rules:** Patient records must be searchable, and appointment status/history must be maintained.
