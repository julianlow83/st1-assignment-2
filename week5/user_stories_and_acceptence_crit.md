| User Story ID | User Story                                                                                                                         | Scenario Type      | Acceptance Criteria (Given - When - Then)                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :--- |:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **US-01** | **As a** Receptionist,<br>**I want to** schedule a new appointment,<br>**so that** patients secure a doctor's available time slot. | Positive           | **Given** a patient exists and a doctor is free at 10:00 AM on 2026-9-15,<br>**When** I enter valid booking details,<br>**Then** the system saves the appointment and displays a success message.                                             |
| **US-02** | **As a** Receptionist,<br>**I want to** block duplicate bookings,<br>**so that** doctors are not double booked.                    | Negative           | **Given** Dr. John Doe is already booked at 10:00 AM on 2026-9-15,<br>**When** I try to book another patient for Dr. John Doe at 10:00 AM on 2026-10-15,<br>**Then** the system rejects the booking and displays a schedule conflict message. |
| **US-03** | **As a** Doctor,<br>**I want to** view my daily schedule,<br>**so that** I know my patient list for the day.                       | Positive           | **Given** multiple appointments exist for Dr. John Doe on 2026-9-15,<br>**When** I filter schedule views by my name and date 2026-9-15,<br>**Then** the system displays all matching appointments in chronological order.                     |
| **US-04** | **As a** Clinic Supervisor,<br>**I want** mandatory input fields enforced,<br>**so that** incomplete records are not saved.        | Negative (Failure) | **Given** I am on the booking screen,<br>**When** I submit an appointment leaving the patient name field blank,<br>**Then** the system blocks creating a new record and shows a mandatory field error.                                        |
